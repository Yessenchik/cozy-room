## Assets

All models are built from scaled cubes in code; no 3D models are downloaded.
Textures (`textures/`): wood floor, wall plaster, furniture wood grain, the rug and the
Saturn picture on the wall are
generated by our own script `tools/make_textures.py` (noise and patterns in numpy, saved
with Pillow). They are our own work; no third-party images are used.
